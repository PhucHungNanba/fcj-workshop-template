# Sharing and Feedback

My 8-week journey in the AWS First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program has been an incredibly transformative experience. It perfectly bridged the gap between the theoretical Information Technology concepts I studied at Saigon University and the practical realities of enterprise cloud architecture.

### Gratitude & Acknowledgements
I would like to express my deepest gratitude to the **AWS mentors and the FCAJ organizing team** for their dedicated guidance and unwavering support. Your insights helped me navigate complex concepts like Serverless computing, infrastructure as code (IaC), and cloud security with ease.

A special thanks goes to my project partner, **Nguyễn Như Vương**. Collaborating on the Electronic Document Management System (EDMS)—from debating architecture choices to auditing IAM policies and debugging deployments together—made this internship vastly more rewarding and enjoyable. 

### Final Thoughts
Building a fully functional, event-driven Serverless application from scratch gave me immense confidence in my system engineering and architecture skills. The hands-on experience with services like AWS Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, and Step Functions has solidified my passion for cloud computing. I am stepping out of this bootcamp not just with technical certifications or new skills, but with a deeper, more strategic understanding of how to build scalable, secure, and cost-efficient solutions.
